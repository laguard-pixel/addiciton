What Is An Addiction?




An addiction is a compulsive need to complete an action or use something. You can be addicted to abusing substances, such as drugs, alcohol, smoking, and sugar. You can also be addicted to doing certain behaviors, such as gambling, shopping, or playing video games. Any type of addiction can prove to be harmful to one’s physical or mental state. Anyone and everyone can suffer from an addiction.

Here in New York City, the most common addictions are to abusing substances, mainly alcohol, crack and cocaine, cannabis, and opioids. (most especially fentanyl and heroin) The borough that has the most overdose deaths in the last few years(2024-2026) is the Bronx with 723 followed by Manhattan with 529 deaths with Brooklyn with 509 deaths.

The most common behavioral addictions for New York City residents is gambling, technology use, and compulsive shopping. (digital and physical).
