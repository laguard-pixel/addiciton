Addiction

What? Causes Problems Solutions

This Article shows what addiction is, the causes and effects of it, the problems with it, and what can be done to prevent it.

What You Can Do To Stop It




Seek medical help by calling a hotline number that can help with addiction. If you are someone you know of that are dealing with addiction problems, call or text 988 Suicide & Crisis to connect with the Suicide and Crisis Hotline. You can also talk to doctors as they are aware of the addiction problems and can provide medical treatments if the symptoms get worse

Use your time wisely and instead of focusing on the addiction habits like smoking or gaming, talk to a family member and have walks. Walking is one of the common ways to help with an addiction since it’s a way to calm your mind with nature.

If your family or friends have noticed your negative changes and called it out, do not deny it. There are people that can get affected by someone who is addicted, and listening to them can help prevent your own addictions and improve your social health.

You can attend education programs to help with addictions. If you start thinking about your careers, keep thinking about it since it is a great way to distract your minds from addictions and boost your hope a little bit higher. When you are in school and attempting to accomplish something good, you can have pride in yourself and the beginning of changing to a better person. If you have school friends that does not have a healthy behaviour, think of changing friends because they can peer pressure you in school to into addictions which is bad for you if you are trying to avoid addiction or getting bad grades.
