This Article shows what addiction is, the causes and effects of it, the problems with it, and what can be done to prevent it.

Common Causes




There are many reasons/causes for addiction but the most common causes are usually from strong emotional feelings such as having fear or guilt, like having an intense argument as an example.

An addiction usually starts because of a hormone called dopamine, which is usually released whenever you, the person, is happy, feeling good, or being rewarded for something.
