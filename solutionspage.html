Addiction

What? Causes Problems Solutions

This Article shows what addiction is, the causes and effects of it, the problems with it, and what can be done to prevent it.

The Short And Long Term Problems




Although addiction can be seen as something not needing to be dealt with immediately, the long term causes are far from being good for the person’s mental health. Some short term problems with addiction can be getting easily distracted from what you’re addicted to and not being able to clearly focus on any other task.

Short terms problems from addictions can be physical, such as breathing, heart rate issues, overdoses from substances, or mental, such as issues with focusing, depression, mood swings, and anxiety.

Some long term problems with addiction are usually not wanting to stop(and/or quit) what you’re addicted to, usually causing major anxiety to kick in once stop doing such addiction, making the person rely on that addiction to keep them happy or busy.

Seek medical help by calling a hotline number that can help with addiction. If you, or someone you know of that are dealing with addiction problems, call or text 988 Suicide & Crisis to connect with the Suicide and Crisis Hotline. You can also talk to doctors as they are aware of the problems with addiction and can provide medical treatment if symptoms worsen.
